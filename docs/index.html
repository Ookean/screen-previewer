ScreenPreview for Windows

A lightweight live preview for any of your monitors, with quick window-to-monitor moving.

What it does

ScreenPreview shows a live, resizable preview of any monitor connected to your PC in a single small window. It's built for setups where a monitor is set to "Extend" and isn't visible on your main screen, so you can see what's on it without physically looking at it or switching inputs. A dropdown lets you jump between monitors, and a second dropdown lets you send any open window straight to a chosen monitor, centered automatically.

Use cases

  • Extra display you can't see — preview a monitor facing away from you, in another room, or otherwise out of view.
  • Tabletop / GM setups — run a secondary "player-facing" display and confirm what it's showing without turning around.
  • Presenting or streaming — check exactly what's on your presentation or capture display before switching to it.
  • Multi-monitor window wrangling — quickly move a stray window onto the right monitor without dragging it across the desktop.
